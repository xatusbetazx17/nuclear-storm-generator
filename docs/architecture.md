# System Architecture

## Modules
- storm_simulator.py: Simulates and visualizes energy storm output.
- energy_converter.py: Converts nuclear input into usable energy (Megawatts).
- ethical_protocol.py: Evaluates system’s decision under ethical frameworks.
- safety_mechanism.py: Emergency stop and fallback protocols.

## Simulated Use-Case
Input: 5000 MW raw nuclear energy  
Output: Controlled storm visual representation (Waveform)
